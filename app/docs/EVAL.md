## Readout (7 Nov)
- With evidence, the engine beats the lab's B2 by +1.3 spec agreement and +2.8 pp full-spec exact. Its biggest gains are length (+5.2 pp), flex (+1.8) and grip (+1.5).
- With answers only, the engine beats today's rules (B1) by +1.1. The gain comes from bias-corrected self-reports, mainly length and flex.
- Purchase (stock vs custom) is 0.4 pp below B2. Lie stays the weakest part (56 %) without a lie test.
- Next steps: tune the fusion SDs and the close-call threshold on a train split if one is released. Do not tune on dev.
