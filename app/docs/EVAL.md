## Readout (7 Nov)
- **Headline (no loft, dev):** with evidence the engine scores 84.75, against 83.58 for the lab's B2 (+1.2). Answers only, it scores 78.06, against 77.10 for today's rules (+1.0). Train shows the same picture: 84.46 vs 83.12, and 77.85 vs 76.93.
- The biggest gain is length (about +5 pp vs B2), then flex and grip. Purchase is about level with B2. Lie stays the weakest part (about 55 %) without a lie test.
- Own loft adds only about 0.1, so it stays an optional question.
- Measurement SDs: the photo and video values come from the lab error levels. Video tempo is 1/fps + 12 ms. TrackMan 1.5, radar 2.0, toe 0.5 and ball-line tilt 2.0 are our assumptions.
- No tuning has been done yet. When it happens, use train only, then report on dev once.
