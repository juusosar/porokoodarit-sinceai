# Evaluation (POR-19)

Synthetic lab data (TAKOMO_FITTING_LAB_2026), never real golfers. The tables below are generated: `python -m eval.dev_split --split dev|train --out ../docs/EVAL.md` rewrites only its own block. Tune on train only; dev is the reporting split.

Own 7-iron loft is an optional question in the web app, so the headline numbers are the runs **without** loft. The +loft rows show what a golfer who knows their loft gets.

**What the shipped app scores:** the E-UI rows feed the engine only what the web app collects today (no photos, radar or lie tests; no age or tempo question; flight skipped for >92 scorers or short carry; tempo from the on-device video). It is still optimistic on two inputs: priorities enter as the lab's clean list (the app reads them from chat text) and height / wrist-to-floor are always given. E2 is the ceiling with every lab measurement, not what the app delivers.

## Readout (7 Nov, evening: recommendation engine inputs)
- **Dev, no loft:** with evidence 85.24 (was 84.75; lab B2 83.58). Answers only 78.73 (was 78.06; today's rules 77.10). Full-spec exact with evidence 30.2 % (was 27.6 %). Shipped web inputs (E-UI) 78.33 (was 77.81), E-UI+LM 79.97 (was 79.66). Train moved the same way: 84.91 (was 84.46) and 78.51 (was 77.85).
- Where it came from: shaft model +4 pp (TrackMan landing angle sets the flight, see `docs/RECOMMENDATION_ENGINE.md`), flex and material +1 to +2 pp (score-bucket speed prior), lie +0.7 pp (ball-line tilt now scaled by k = -0.65 instead of taken 1:1). Head moved -0.2 pp.
- Tuned on train only: ball-line k, b, SD (grid, lie accuracy on golfers whose only lie evidence is the ball-line test: 39.8 % with the old 1:1 mapping, 43.5 % tuned, 33.7 % chart only); score prior SD (6 / 8.5 / 12 / none; 12 best); flight and launch fits are least squares on train TrackMan sessions.
- This run used pandas 2.3.3 (earlier tables: pandas 3.1.1). The B1/B2 rows are identical, so the scorer is unaffected.

## Readout (7 Nov)
- **Shipped app (dev, synthetic):** E-UI scores 77.81 vs 77.10 for today's questionnaire (+0.7); with a launch monitor speed (golfers who have one) 79.66 (+2.6). Without the optional club-speed slider it was 76.20, below today's tool: speed-reporters had no way to give speed. Use these numbers, not E2, when describing the product.
- The gap to E2 (84.75) is the measurements the app doesn't collect yet: body/hand photo (length, grip), video club speed and a lie test (lie stays ~42 %).
- **Engine ceiling (no loft, dev):** with every lab measurement the engine scores 84.75, against 83.58 for the lab's B2 (+1.2). Answers only, it scores 78.06, against 77.10 for today's rules (+1.0). Train shows the same picture: 84.46 vs 83.12, and 77.85 vs 76.93.
- The biggest gain is length (about +5 pp vs B2), then flex and grip. Purchase is about level with B2. Lie stays the weakest part (about 55 %) without a lie test.
- Own loft adds only about 0.1, so it stays an optional question.
- Measurement SDs: the photo and video values come from the lab error levels. Video tempo is 1/fps + 12 ms. TrackMan 1.5, radar 2.0, toe 0.5 and ball-line tilt 2.0 are our assumptions.
- No tuning has been done yet. When it happens, use train only, then report on dev once.
